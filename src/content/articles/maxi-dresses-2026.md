The maxi dress has a reputation problem. It gets filed under holiday wear, or summer occasion dressing, or the kind of thing you reach for when nothing else is clean. None of that does justice to what it can actually do — which is cover more occasions, across more seasons, than almost any other dress length.

The difficulty is finding the right one. A poorly cut maxi can overwhelm rather than ease a getting-dressed decision. The wrong fabric collapses by lunchtime or sticks in heat. The wrong length catches on every surface. Getting it right requires a degree of specificity that most searches don't provide. endource's edit of over 900 maxi dresses spans Ghost, Ganni, Missoni, Khaite, Zimmermann, AllSaints, Rixo, Victoria Beckham and more — covering everything from relaxed everyday pieces through to formal evening gowns. ## The everyday maxi

The case for the everyday maxi is simpler than it sounds: a well-made one in the right fabric requires less styling than almost any other dress. No decisions about tights, no working out whether the length is right for the context. You put it on and the proportion takes care of itself.

**Ghost** is the most represented brand in this part of the edit, and it makes sense. The brand's satin maxis — the Delphine, the Elara Cowl Neck, the Arabella Empire Line, the Nora Ruffled Shoulder, the River Sleeveless — are all built around the same logic: a fluid, lightweight satin that moves well, holds its shape across a long day, and works for more occasions than the fabric might suggest. The cowl neck and empire line cuts in particular are useful shapes — they flatter a wide range of proportions without requiring the dress to do anything showy. **Ghost** satin maxis photograph and wear equally well, which makes them reliable choices for occasions where you're uncertain about the dress code.

**AllSaints** contributes a large selection of more relaxed maxi dresses. The Sienna Ruffle Maxi Dress is the most distinctive — a tiered ruffle construction in a fluid fabric that creates movement without bulk. AllSaints maxi dresses tend to work well for casual occasions and travel, where the length is practical rather than formal.

**Hush's** Winslow Air Flow Maxi Dress makes the case for the everyday maxi in summer: a lightweight fabric cut loosely enough to work in heat, with a simple neckline that suits a wide range of occasions. **Albaray** contributes several well-priced maxi pieces — the Smock Spot Maxi Dress and the Paisley Tie Neck Maxi Dress are both good examples of the brand's approach: modest price, decent fabric, practical construction. The Long Sleeve Godet Maxi Dress is the most useful of the Albaray pieces for cooler months, where a long sleeve and godet skirt create something that works from September through March.

**Phase Eight** has a large number of pieces in the edit. The Marcie Textured Bodice Maxi Dress and the Orla Sleeveless Square Neck Floral Midi Dress both represent the brand's practical approach to occasion wear — dresses designed to work at a smart lunch or a family celebration without requiring a significant outlay or significant thought.

## The printed maxi

Print is one of the things the maxi length does best. At floor length, a print has room to develop in a way that a midi or mini doesn't allow. The scale works differently; patterns that might look busy at a shorter length can look deliberate and considered at maxi.

**Ganni** has fourteen pieces in the edit, and several of them make this case well. The Ruched Floral-Print Stretch-Mesh Maxi Dress is the most relaxed — a gathered mesh in a floral print that works for summer occasions. The Ruched Printed Stretch-Mesh Midi Dress takes the same approach at a shorter length. Both pieces carry the Greener badge, indicating more considered production.

**Ulla Johnson** contributes several printed silk pieces that represent the brand at its most characteristic. The Lali Ruched Ruffled Printed Silk Maxi Dress is the standout — silk in a floral print with ruched and ruffled detailing that gives the dress movement and presence. Ulla Johnson's approach to print is distinctive: the colours tend to be slightly unexpected, the scale of the pattern well-judged. These are dresses that look better in person than in product photography, which is always a reliable indicator.

**Maje** has a number of printed maxi pieces — the Cutout Printed Georgette Maxi Dress and the Cutout Printed Silk-Twill Maxi Dress both use cutout detailing to give the print an additional point of interest. The Printed Satin Maxi Dress is more conventional but well-made. **Sandro's** Crystal-Embellished Floral Twill Maxi Dress uses embellishment alongside print to create something that works for smarter occasions.

**Victoria Beckham** has multiple printed maxi pieces in the edit, including the Printed Silk Crepe De Chine Maxi Dress and the Printed Crepe Gown. Both represent the brand's characteristic approach: restrained palette, precise cut, fabric quality that justifies the outlay. The silk crepe de chine version is among the better investment pieces in the edit for anyone who wears printed maxi dresses regularly.

**Melissa Odabash's** Juniper Pleated Floral-Print Mousseline Midi Dress sits at the edge of the maxi category but worth including: the mousseline fabric, the pleating, and the floral print combine to create something that works for occasions from garden parties to beach holidays.

## The occasion maxi

The full-length dress for formal occasions is having something of a moment — partly because the midi has become so ubiquitous that a full-length dress now looks more deliberate. For black tie, formal dinners, or weddings where you want to be properly dressed, the maxi gown is the clearest answer.

**Missoni** contributes a substantial number of pieces that make the case for knitwear at full length. The Metallic Knitted Maxi Wrap Dress and the One-Shoulder Metallic Ribbed-Knit Maxi Dress are both evening pieces — the metallic yarn catching light in a way that works for occasion wear without requiring sequins or embellishment. The Striped Knitted Maxi Dress and the Metallic Argyle Intarsia-Knit Maxi Dress are more daytime but still work for smart occasions. Missoni's knitwear at this length is distinctive in the edit — there's nothing else like it.

**Khaite** has fourteen pieces across the edit. The Leif Gathered Twill Maxi Dress is the most versatile — a gathered construction in twill that works for occasions ranging from a smart lunch to a formal dinner. Khaite's maxi dresses are built around precise proportioning; the length, the gather placement, the neckline height are all calibrated to work without additional styling.

**Zimmermann** has twenty pieces in the edit. The Gathered Velvet Maxi Dress is a winter occasion piece — velvet at floor length is unambiguously formal, and Zimmermann's construction is among the best in this category. The Tiered Pointelle-Knit Maxi Dress and the Lace-Trimmed Floral Linen Maxi Dress are both more relaxed, with the linen piece particularly suited to warm weather occasions.

**Victoria Beckham's** Pleated Satin-Crepe Halterneck Maxi Dress is among the most carefully resolved occasion pieces in the edit — the halterneck creates a formal neckline, the pleated satin-crepe has a weight and movement that works at full length, and the overall effect is of something arrived at with care. **Roksanda** has eight pieces in the edit, several of which suit the more formal end of occasion dressing. **Valentino Garavani** contributes a number of archival gowns at significant reductions — pieces for occasions where nothing else will do.

**Joseph** has twenty-five pieces across the edit. The brand's maxi dresses tend toward the minimal — precise cut, quality fabric, very little decoration. They're pieces for occasions where you want the fabric and the proportion to do the work. **Stella McCartney's** Asymmetric Draped Gown takes a similar approach but with a more dramatic silhouette — the asymmetric drape creates a specific kind of formal presence.

## The knitted maxi

One of the underused expressions of the maxi length is the knitted dress. The argument for it is simple: a knitted maxi in the right weight works from September through April in a way that no satin or linen alternative can manage. It's the maxi dress as a year-round proposition rather than a seasonal one.

**Missoni's** pieces make the strongest case here, as noted above. But **Jacquemus** also contributes to this category — the Noeud One-Shoulder Knitted Midi Dress sits at the edge of the maxi category, but the brand's approach to knitwear length is worth attention. **Bite Studios** has fifteen pieces in the edit, and the brand's focus on thoughtful production and natural fibres makes it a strong option for knitted pieces. **TOVE** has five pieces — the brand's approach to the knitted maxi tends toward the architectural, with structured silhouettes in neutral colours that work across contexts.

**Frame** contributes a number of jersey pieces. The Cutout Maxi Dress is a jersey piece with cutout detailing — a relaxed option that works for casual occasions. Frame's maxi dresses tend to be practical rather than decorative, which makes them useful for travel and everyday wearing.

## What to look for

Hem length matters more than most people account for when buying a maxi dress. True floor-length — where the hem grazes or touches the floor — is a different garment to ankle-length, and both are different to what's sometimes called a midi-maxi, which falls somewhere between the knee and the calf. True floor-length works best for formal occasions; ankle-length is more practical for everyday; midi-maxi covers the widest range of occasions.

Fabric determines season. Linen, cotton, and lightweight jersey are summer fabrics that lose their point in cooler months. Silk and satin are year-round with the right layering. Knit, velvet, and heavier crepe work from autumn through spring. The Ghost satin pieces in this edit are deceptively year-round — the fabric is light enough for summer but warm enough to wear with a coat in autumn.

The neckline of a maxi dress does disproportionate work given the length of the garment. A cowl neck (Ghost, Victoria Beckham) creates a soft drape that sits well from the front. A halterneck (Victoria Beckham, Simkhai) is specifically an evening choice. A square neck (Phase Eight) is the most versatile — it photographs well and works across occasions.
