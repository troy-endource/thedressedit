The difference between a smart shopper and everyone else isn't willpower or a bigger budget — it's timing. The exact same dress that costs £180 in September will be £90 in January and £70 if you know when the flash sale lands. Multiply that across a year's worth of dresses and you're saving hundreds of pounds without sacrificing quality, brand, or style.

This calendar is your cheat sheet. Month by month, we cover which UK retailers hold sales when, what discounts to expect, and what's worth buying at each point in the year. We update it regularly as sale dates are confirmed, so bookmark this page and check back before any purchase.

## Golden Rules of Sale Shopping

Before we get to the calendar, three rules that will save you money and wardrobe space:

**Rule 1: Only buy what you'd buy at full price.** The single biggest mistake in sale shopping is buying something you don't love because it's reduced. A £40 dress you never wear is more expensive than a £120 dress you wear twenty times. Before clicking "add to bag," ask: would I buy this today at full price? If not, close the tab.

**Rule 2: Know your sizes across brands.** Sales move fast, and popular sizes disappear within hours. If you already know you're a 10 in COS, a 12 in & Other Stories, and an M in Mango, you can act quickly when prices drop. Keep a note in your phone.

**Rule 3: Check the returns policy.** Most UK retailers offer full refunds on sale items, but some only offer exchanges or credit notes. Check before you buy, especially for higher-value pieces.

## The Email Newsletter Strategy

This is genuinely the most valuable tip in this entire guide: sign up for email newsletters from the 5–10 brands you shop most. Here's why:

Most brands offer **10–15% off your first order** when you sign up. That's an immediate saving you can use right now. Beyond that, newsletter subscribers typically get **early access to sales** (sometimes 24–48 hours before the general public), **flash sale alerts** that aren't advertised on social media, and **exclusive discount codes** during quiet retail periods.

The brands where newsletter signup is most valuable: COS (their sale is genuinely excellent), & Other Stories, Reiss (regular 20% off events for members), Whistles, and M&S Sparks (loyalty points plus regular targeted discounts).

## The Month-by-Month Calendar

### January: Post-Christmas Clearance

**What's happening:** The biggest sales of the year. Retailers are clearing autumn/winter stock at deep discounts to make room for spring arrivals.

**Typical discounts:** 40–70% off autumn/winter collections. Some retailers go to 80% off in final clearance.

**Best retailers this month:** COS (excellent reductions on knitwear and winter dresses), Reiss (30–50% off), & Other Stories (up to 60% off), M&S (clearance across all ranges), Mango (aggressive discounting, especially online).

**What to buy:** Winter work dresses, dark-coloured midis, heavier fabrics. Also an excellent time to buy classic pieces (a navy COS dress, a Reiss blazer dress) that work year-round. Avoid buying summer dresses — stock is limited and you'll get better options in a few months.

### February: Quiet Period (Hidden Gems)

**What's happening:** The retail dead zone. Christmas sales are winding down, spring stock is starting to arrive, and retailers are in transition. This is actually a great time to shop if you know where to look.

**Typical discounts:** End-of-sale final clearance (60–80% off remaining winter stock). Early spring pieces at full price.

**Best retailers this month:** H&M and Mango often have surprise mid-season flash sales. ASOS runs regular promotions. John Lewis clearance can have excellent finds.

**What to buy:** The last of the winter sale at rock-bottom prices. Also the first spring pieces if you want new-season stock before everyone else.

### March: Spring Sales Begin

**What's happening:** Spring has arrived (in theory), and retailers are pushing new-season collections. Many run targeted promotions to drive traffic — 20% off new arrivals, "spring into savings" events, and early Easter sales.

**Typical discounts:** 15–25% off new spring stock (via codes and promotions). Remaining winter items at 70%+ off.

**Best retailers this month:** Reiss often runs a "friends and family" event (20% off everything). M&S has spring promotions. & Other Stories and COS refresh their sale sections with new markdowns.

**What to buy:** Wedding guest dresses. This is the optimal time — the widest selection of new-season dresses, with enough promotions to take the edge off the price. Summer work dresses are also worth buying now while sizing is complete.

### April: Easter Sales

**What's happening:** The Easter bank holiday weekend is a major UK retail event. Expect site-wide promotions, free delivery offers, and targeted discounts.

**Typical discounts:** 20–30% off across many retailers. Some run 15% off full-price items alongside deeper sale reductions.

**Best retailers this month:** Almost everyone runs something over Easter. John Lewis, Next, M&S, Reiss, and most high street brands participate.

**What to buy:** Anything you need for the summer season. This is your last chance to buy popular styles in your size before wedding season depletes stock.

### May: Wedding Season Begins (Full Price Territory)

**What's happening:** Retailers know you need a dress and they're not in a hurry to discount. May through mid-July is the most expensive time to buy occasion dresses. Stock levels are high, but so are prices.

**Typical discounts:** Minimal. You'll find small promotions but nothing major.

**What to buy:** If you didn't buy during March–April sales, you'll be paying full price. Focus on pieces you genuinely love and will rewear.

### June–July: Mid-Season Sales

**What's happening:** The first cracks appear in summer pricing. Retailers run mid-season sales to maintain momentum, clearing early-summer stock and making room for late-season arrivals.

**Typical discounts:** 20–40% off spring/summer collections. COS, & Other Stories, and Mango typically start their summer sale in late June.

**Best retailers this month:** COS summer sale (excellent), Mango (aggressive online discounting), & Other Stories (great reductions on printed dresses), ASOS (regular 20% off events).

**What to buy:** Summer dresses for August and September weddings. Smart-casual work dresses for the warmer months. Holiday pieces.

### August: Summer Clearance

**What's happening:** Deep summer sales as retailers start clearing for autumn. If you're flexible on colour and style, this is one of the best value months of the year.

**Typical discounts:** 40–60% off remaining summer stock. Some items reach 70% off.

**Best retailers this month:** Almost all high street and mid-range brands are in sale. COS, Reiss, Whistles, Mango, and & Other Stories all have significant markdowns.

**What to buy:** Summer dresses for next year (if you spot a classic), transitional pieces that work into autumn, and lighter-weight work dresses.

### September–October: Autumn Arrives (Transitional Deals)

**What's happening:** New autumn collections are launching at full price, but summer clearance continues in the background. A good time for strategic shopping.

**Typical discounts:** Final summer clearance (60%+) alongside full-price autumn. Some brands run "new season" promotions of 10–15% off.

**What to buy:** Versatile midi dresses that work across seasons — heavier jersey, knit dresses, and darker colours that carry through to winter.

### November: Black Friday & Cyber Monday

**What's happening:** The second-biggest discount event of the year, and increasingly the most useful for strategic dress buying. Many retailers now run promotions for the full week or even the full month.

**Typical discounts:** 20–40% off full-price items. Some brands go higher. Premium brands that rarely discount (Reiss, Whistles, COS) often participate.

**Best retailers this month:** Reiss (their Black Friday sale is genuinely excellent — 30% off everything), COS, & Other Stories, AllSaints, M&S, John Lewis.

**What to buy:** Investment pieces. This is the time to buy the Reiss work dress or the COS coat you've been eyeing. Also an excellent time to buy ahead for next year's wedding season — stock a few versatile dresses at 30% off now, and you'll thank yourself in June.

### December: Christmas Sales & Early January

**What's happening:** Pre-Christmas promotions throughout the first three weeks, then the Boxing Day / post-Christmas sales that bleed into January's clearance.

**Typical discounts:** 15–20% in early December promotions, then 30–60% in Boxing Day sales.

**What to buy:** Party dresses (for obvious reasons), plus any investment pieces you missed during Black Friday.

## The Bottom Line

The single most important thing to remember: the best time to buy a dress is before you need it. If you know you have weddings in summer, buy in March. If you need work dresses for autumn, buy in August sales. Planning one season ahead consistently saves 20–40% — and you get the best selection because popular sizes haven't sold out.
