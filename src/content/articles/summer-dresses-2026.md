Summer dressing in Britain comes with a particular set of demands. The occasion shifts — a garden party here, a week abroad there, a warm Friday evening that turns into something more — and so does the weather, sometimes within the same afternoon. The dress that works for all of it tends to be one you didn't overthink: the right fabric, a considered cut, and enough versatility to carry you from one context to the next without changing.

endource's summer dresses edit brings together over 600 options from Ganni and Jigsaw to Zimmermann and Toteme, filtered for quality and longevity rather than trend. Whether you're looking for something easy for everyday wear or a more considered piece for a specific occasion, this is where to start.

## The everyday dress

The most useful summer dress is the one that doesn't require a decision. You reach for it, it works, and you stop thinking about it. For most people, that's a shirt dress or a relaxed midi in a natural fabric — something that reads put-together without trying.

Linen is the fabric that performs best in warm weather: it breathes, it softens with wear, and it improves rather than deteriorates over a season. Jigsaw's Linen Pleat Front Shirt Dress in red is a good example of the form — the pleat detail adds structure without formality, and the belted waist gives it enough shape that it doesn't need styling beyond flat sandals. Polo Ralph Lauren's Linen Panelled Shirtdress takes a similar approach with more considered construction, the panelling giving it a tailored quality that works as well on a warm day in the office as it does elsewhere.

For those who prefer a looser silhouette, Hush's Liddie Air Flow Maxi Dress — made with Lenzing EcoVero viscose — offers the kind of easy, relaxed drape that moves well in heat. Gerard Darel's belted shirt dresses, the Ralia and the Rana, are also worth noting: both are cut with the ease of French daywear and come in the kind of neutral tones that make them genuinely versatile across settings.

A V-neck jersey dress is worth keeping in the category too. Boden's Cary V-Neck Jersey Dress in blood orange is exactly the kind of piece that earns its place in a summer wardrobe — simple enough to wear constantly, the colour doing the work that cut alone can't.

## The holiday dress

Holiday dressing asks something different: more colour, more print, less structure. The dresses that travel best are lightweight, packable, and confident enough in themselves that they don't need much else. A printed maxi or a botanical midi tends to answer this better than a considered neutral.

Zimmermann has the strongest offering in the edit for this purpose. The Rebellion Tie-Detailed Ruffled Printed Chiffon Maxi Dress is the kind of piece that looks better against a warm background — the print does serious work, the ruffled hem and tie waist giving it movement without fuss. If that sits beyond what you'd spend on holiday, the Luna Shirred Lantern Linen-Silk Mini Dress offers a more restrained take on Zimmermann's sensibility at a lower entry point, and the linen-silk blend travels remarkably well.

Dôen's Emmaretta Shirred Floral-Print Organic Cotton Midi Dress represents the same holiday spirit with a somewhat softer palette — the shirred bodice means fit is forgiving across sizes, and organic cotton in warm weather is considerably more comfortable than synthetics. Faithfull's Sofia Floral-Print Linen Mini Dress covers similar ground in a shorter silhouette, with the kind of botanical print that reads intentional rather than generic.

For those who find resort dressing veers too romantic, Staud's Lorena Open-Back Striped Knitted Maxi Dress offers a different register entirely — the open back and stripe pattern work especially well in evening settings, and knit in this weight feels more considered than cotton for warmer nights.

When it comes to necklines for holiday, halternecks and V-necks earn their place: they balance bare arms and shoulders without overcomplicating things, and tend to sit better under sunlight than high or structured necklines.

## The garden party dress

The garden party dress sits between casual and occasion wear, and getting it wrong in either direction is easy. Too formal and it reads like you've misread the invitation; too relaxed and it doesn't acknowledge the occasion at all. The sweet spot is a dress with clear intent — a print or a fabric that signals the occasion without requiring accessories to complete it.

Floral midis work well here, particularly in prints that feel considered rather than generic. Phase Eight's Nita Floral Tiered Maxi Dress in cream multi has the volume and the colour palette for a summer gathering — the tiered construction means it moves well, and the maxi length removes any concern about seating. The Caterina Floral Midi Dress and the Orla Sleeveless Square-Neck Floral Midi Dress from the same brand cover similar territory at slightly different lengths; both are made with sustainability credentials and both hold their shape through a long afternoon.

Gerard Darel's Romaine Floral Dress in ecru, and the Romie Flora Tiered Mini Dress in blue sky, offer a slightly more French-inflected take — the prints are smaller and more contained, the silhouettes less voluminous. These work particularly well if the setting is a country garden rather than a marquee.

Saloni's Fleur Printed Satin Halterneck Gown is the considered choice for those who want something more formal — the halterneck and the printed satin read unmistakably as occasion wear, without veering into the territory of wedding guest dressing. Erdem's Floral-Print Flared Silk Midi Dress is similarly positioned: the flare in the skirt is generous, the silk print detailed, and it's the kind of dress that people remember.

For a garden party that might run into a warm evening, it's worth thinking about how a dress handles a change in temperature. A midi or maxi with sleeves, or a silhouette that layers easily over something simple, tends to serve better than a strapless or a structured mini.

## The occasion dress

Summer has more formal occasions than any other season — weddings, races, charity dinners, long-standing invitations that have been in the diary since January. The occasion dress asks for something that reads unambiguously considered: a fabric with presence, a silhouette that has been thought about, a colour that works for the specific setting.

Lauren Ralph Lauren's georgette gowns — the Floral Ruffle-Trim Georgette Halter Gown and the Floral Ruffle-Trim Georgette Tiered Gown — handle this well. Both are full-length, both have the kind of print and silhouette that communicate occasion without becoming costume. The halterneck version works slightly better in heat; the tiered version offers more movement.

Stella McCartney's Bunch of Roses Long-Sleeve Mesh Midi Dress occupies the more directional end of the category — the mesh fabric and the rose print are unmistakably intentional, and the long sleeves give it a formality that shorter, sleeveless alternatives can't quite match. It's the dress that rewards confidence.

For occasion dressing, fabric matters considerably more than it does for everyday wear. Satin, silk, georgette, and fine mesh all have a quality of surface that reads occasion — cotton and linen, however beautiful, tend to read as casual in formal settings regardless of the silhouette.

L.K. Bennett's Mimi Floral Pure Silk Dress, currently in the edit at a reduced price, is worth noting here — pure silk in a considered print, cut for a midi length that works across occasion types.
